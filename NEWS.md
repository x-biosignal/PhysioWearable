# PhysioWearable 0.1.1

## Validation

- Added GGIR gold-standard validation (VAL-10). `computeENMO()` is checked
  against GGIR's ENMO (`GGIR::g.applymetrics`) on a real Axivity AX3 device
  recording (from GGIRread), reproducing it to numerical precision; the GGIR
  reference is bundled (`tests/testthat/fixtures/wearable-ggir-reference.rds`,
  regenerated by `data-raw/wearable_ggir_reference.R`) and the raw file is read
  from GGIRread at test time. `autoCalibrateAccel()` (van Hees et al. 2014) is
  validated by ground-truth recovery of a known miscalibration from
  sphere-covering still data and cross-checked against an independent algebraic
  ellipsoid fit. GGIR/GGIRread added to Suggests.
